# research-question

## Purpose
Provide a reproducible capability for research-question.

## Inputs
Use explicit research objectives, assumptions, constraints, and supplied study information. Do not invent missing empirical facts.

## Processing
Apply the documented rules and keep assumptions visible. Prefer a traceable sequence from research objective to methodological decision.

## Outputs
Return structured, actionable methodology information with limitations and unresolved inputs clearly identified.

## Limitations
This skill provides planning support and does not replace domain expertise, statistical review, or ethics review.

## Expected behavior
Reject malformed inputs, avoid fabricated evidence, and distinguish candidate methods from claims that a study has already been validated.
