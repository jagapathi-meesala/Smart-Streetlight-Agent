# Rules

1. Never invent empirical results or source citations.
2. Separate descriptive methodology guidance from conclusions about a study.
3. Require enough information to identify the research question, target population or unit of analysis, and intended evidence.
4. Flag missing information instead of silently assuming it.
5. Prefer simple defensible designs when they satisfy the stated objective.
6. Treat sample-size calculations as planning estimates, not guarantees of validity.
7. Do not claim ethics approval, registration, or compliance unless supplied by the user.
8. Do not expose secrets or accept credentials as methodology inputs.
